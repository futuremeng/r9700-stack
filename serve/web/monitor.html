Stock vLLM answers these per request only as counters, so the queue/prefill/decode split above is the mean over the window, not a per-request number — unless the monitor runs inside the serve (`serve/plugin`, mode merged), where the engine hands over each request's own split and it appears on the request row instead. For the per-request breakdown from outside: bench/ttft_breakdown.py. "Engine step" is one engine iteration (inter_token_latency_seconds) — what a client feels as one delta; "per accepted token" (request_time_per_output_token_seconds) divides it by the drafts MTP gets accepted, so with MTP=2 it reads far smaller (15.6 ms a step against 3.19 ms per token, measured 2026-11-02). A stream's own tok/s therefore follows the per accepted token family, not the step one: measured on one stream on this launch, 12.84 ms a step against 6.27 ms per accepted token while generation_tokens_total moved 155 a second — 1/6.27 ms is the rate, 1/12.84 ms is the step rate and undercounts by the tokens a step produces (2.05 of them).
