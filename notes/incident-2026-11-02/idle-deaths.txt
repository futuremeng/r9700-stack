## what this rules out

The `notes/native-4xr9700.md` thread (C22/C23) is about slow host-side work in the load path. Death 7 puts a
sync flood + fatal cache-level MCE on a box with no GPU job, no PCIe traffic worth the name and 0.12 load, so
the fault domain is not the load path: the same signature appears at idle and at full model load. What is
still common to all seven is *the box being up and running Linux on this platform* -- and death 4 (`3b531c63`,
32 s of life, no firmware latch) is inside the recovery of death 3, so a death can be followed by another
death within half a minute of boot with nothing running at all.
